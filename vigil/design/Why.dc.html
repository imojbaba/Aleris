We live as if there is always tomorrow.

Mostly there is. But the photographs, the accounts, the thing you keep meaning to tell her — all of it assumes one more chance to sort it out.

Vigil holds them for you, sealed, until you stop answering. Then it gives them to the people you chose.

Everything is locked on your phone before it reaches us. We hold it. We cannot read it.

Takes about ten minutes. You can stop anywhere.
